Privacy Policy

Cathedral Home Assistant · Effective September 24, 2026

Cathedral Home Assistant ("the app") is a private tool used by a single household with its own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how that data is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (read): event titles, dates, and times from calendars the household has chosen. Descriptions, locations, attachments, and guest email addresses are not requested.
  • Google Calendar (edit owned events): the app creates or updates events on calendars owned by the signed-in account, and only when a household member asks it to.
  • Gmail (read): messages from a short list of specific sender addresses. The app keeps only the lines that mention dates, times, or events and discards the rest of the message. Attachments are not downloaded.
  • Gmail (compose): the app saves drafts for a household member to review. The app's code allows sending from one designated account only. It never deletes, archives, labels, or forwards mail and never changes mailbox settings.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the household's own scheduling and drafting features to the account owners who authorized it. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with data brokers or other third parties.

Processing happens on the household's own computer, using a locally installed AI model by default. A cloud AI service receives the content of a request only when a household member chooses one for that specific request. Google user data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are kept in the computer's operating-system keychain. The limited calendar and email extracts are stored on that computer in files readable only by its owner. Nothing is stored on a public server.

Limited Use

Cathedral Home Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Any account owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. On request, the household operator deletes the locally stored data for that account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in consent screen.
