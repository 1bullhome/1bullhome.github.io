Cathedral Home Assistant

A private household tool. It is not a public service and is not open for sign-up.

What it is

Cathedral Home Assistant runs on one household's own computer and helps that household keep its schedule organized. It works only with Google accounts that belong to the household. Each account owner signs in and approves access individually.

What it does with Google

  • Google Calendar: it reads upcoming events to build a weekly family schedule. It can also add or update events on calendars the signed-in account owns.
  • Gmail: it reads messages from a short list of specific senders and keeps only the lines that mention dates, times, or events. It can also save email drafts for a person to review. Sending is limited in the app's own code to one designated account.

See the privacy policy for how this information is handled.
